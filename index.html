01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

ABOUT ME

ENGINEERING MIND.
DESIGN OBSESSION.

✳✦

I like understanding how things work—and imagining how they could work better.

I’m Dishant, a mechanical engineering student with a growing interest in web design and creative technology. Engineering draws me to structure and problem-solving. Design gives me room to explore feeling, identity, and the small details that make something memorable.

Building websites is where those interests meet. I’m drawn to clear layouts, thoughtful interactions, and turning a loose idea into something people can actually explore. My personal projects are a space to experiment, learn, and refine that approach.

I’m still learning, and I want my work to show that progress: more considered decisions, stronger execution, and the curiosity to keep making things.

02 / THE PROCESS

HOW I
CREATE.

Good-looking is a start.
Making sense is what makes it work.

01

Understand the idea.

Start with the purpose, the people, and what they need to find. A clear idea gives every design choice a reason.

02

Make the details count.

Give typography, spacing, colour, and interactions the attention they deserve. Small choices shape the whole experience.

03

Build. Learn. Refine.

Turn the idea into something usable, question what feels unclear, and keep improving through making.
